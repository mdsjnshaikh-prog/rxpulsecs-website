Terms

Important terms for using RxPulse.

Rules for doctor accounts, patient records, prescription tools, and responsible use.

Doctor account

Doctor account creation occurs inside the RxPulse Windows application. Full use requires email code verification, professional information, and administrator approval.

Professional information

Doctors must provide correct BMDC, degree, specialty, and chamber information. If information is wrong or misleading, RxPulse may decline approval, ask for correction, limit use, or stop the account.

Clinical responsibility

RxPulse helps with prescriptions, patient records, printing, and supportive warnings. It does not replace the doctor’s clinical judgment or professional responsibility.

Medicine warnings

ADE or interaction warnings are advisory support only. They are not a complete medical database and do not guarantee detection of every clinical risk.

Patient information and privacy

Patient information should be used professionally. Doctors are responsible for confidentiality when printing, sharing, exporting, or backing up patient information.

Offline use and backup

Work may continue on the local device during weak internet. Until sync succeeds, data is not cloud backup.

Not for emergency care

RxPulse is a documentation and prescription support tool. It should not be relied on for emergency medical decisions or life-saving treatment.

Support

For support, write from your registered email to support@rxpulsecs.com. Do not use RxPulse support for clinical emergencies.
