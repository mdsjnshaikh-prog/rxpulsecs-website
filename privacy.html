Privacy Policy

How RxPulse uses and protects information.

How doctor account, professional, patient, and support information is handled—in simple terms.

Information we collect

We collect information needed for doctor account creation, email verification, approval, subscription, payment review, and support. This may include email, name, BMDC number, degree, specialty, chamber information, payment reference, and support messages.

Patient information

Inside the RxPulse app, doctors may store patient name, age, sex, vital signs, history, diagnosis, prescription, allergy, follow-up notes, and other clinical notes. This information is used only for the relevant doctor and approved system processes. The public website does not collect patient records.

Doctor responsibility

Doctors are responsible for keeping their login, password, device, and exported backup files safe. Doctors must avoid sharing patient information with the wrong person or in unsafe places.

Offline and local data

When internet is weak, RxPulse may keep some information locally first. Until sync succeeds, that information is not cloud backup.

Medicine warnings and clinical support

RxPulse may show supportive warnings about ADE or interactions. These are advisory only; final decisions remain the doctor’s responsibility.

How we use information

Information is used to operate accounts, review approval and payment, provide support, improve security, support sync/backup, and improve the software. We do not sell patient information for advertising.

Information safety

RxPulse uses account access, database rules, and admin review processes to help protect information. No internet-based system is completely risk-free.

Contact

For privacy questions, write from your registered email to support@rxpulsecs.com.
