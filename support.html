Common questions

FAQ

What should I include for faster help?

Your registered email, a short description of the issue, any error message, and Windows version. Do not write clinical emergencies here.

I did not receive the email code

Check spam/junk. Wait a few minutes and try again. If it still does not arrive, email support@rxpulsecs.com.

How long does approval take?

Administrator review is required. Timing can vary. If it is delayed, write from your registered email to ask about status.

Where do I see pricing?

Exact amounts and plans are shown inside the RxPulse app. This website only describes plan types in general.

For urgent patient care?

No. RxPulse support is not for clinical emergencies or patient-specific urgent care.
